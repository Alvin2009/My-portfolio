Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

This website is a personal portfolio. It has no user accounts, no contact form, no newsletter and no analytics. This policy explains what information can reach me through it and through the third-party services it uses.

Who I am

This website is operated by Alvin Emmanuel Gideon, trading as Codex Designs, based in Lagos State, Nigeria. For privacy questions, contact me at alvingideon380@gmail.com.

What information I collect, and why

Information you choose to send me. The site has no form. If you email me, call me or message me on WhatsApp, I receive whatever you include, such as your name, email address, phone number and the content of your message. I use it only to reply to you, discuss possible work and keep a record of that correspondence.

Information collected automatically. The website itself does not run analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and it does not set cookies or store data in your browser. Like any website, the server that delivers the pages can receive technical details of each request (for example your IP address, browser type and the page requested), and the hosting provider may keep standard server logs. I do not use these details to identify you.

Third parties that receive information

  • Google Fonts (Google). The pages load the Manrope and Newsreader typefaces from Google’s servers, so your browser sends Google a request that includes your IP address and browser details.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare). The 3D laptop on the home page uses the three.js library, loaded from Cloudflare’s cdnjs service, which likewise receives your IP address and browser details.
  • Hosting provider. The company that hosts this site processes the requests described above.
  • Email, phone and messaging services. If you use the Email, phone or WhatsApp links, you leave this site and your message is handled by Gmail (Google), your phone network or WhatsApp (Meta) under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • ESTORA Suites. The project link opens a separate website (estorasuites.netlify.app) that has its own hosting and practices.

I do not sell your personal information.

International transfers

Google, Cloudflare, Meta and other providers operate internationally, so information handled by them may be processed outside Nigeria. Their own policies describe the safeguards they apply.

How long I keep information

I have not set a fixed retention period. I keep correspondence only for as long as it is needed to respond to you, carry out any agreed work and keep a reasonable record. You can ask me to delete it (see below).

How information is protected

The site is delivered over HTTPS where the hosting provider supports it, collects no data through forms and loads only the third-party resources listed above. Messages you send by email or WhatsApp are protected by those services. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, so I cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your rights and choices

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and other laws that may apply to you, you may have rights to access your personal data, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, receive a copy of it, and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. To exercise any of these, email alvingideon380@gmail.com. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

You can limit the third-party requests described above by using a browser extension or setting that blocks external fonts and scripts, although the site’s fonts and 3D laptop may then not display.

Children

This website is not aimed at children, and I do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Cookies

See the Cookie Policy for details.

Changes to this policy

If the website changes in a way that affects personal information, for example by adding a contact form or analytics, I will update this policy and, where required, ask for consent first. The date at the top shows when it was last revised.
