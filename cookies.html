Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

In short: this website’s own code does not set cookies and does not store information in your browser. It has no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, so there is no cookie consent banner to manage.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website can save on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage and tracking pixels, can also remember or collect information about how you use a site.

What this website uses

TechnologyUsed here?
First-party cookiesNo
Local storage, session storage or IndexedDBNo
Analytics, advertising or social media tracking toolsNo
Embedded videos, maps or chat widgetsNo

I checked the website’s source code and found no use of cookies or browser storage. I cannot see or control any technical cookies a hosting provider might set, and I have not found any. Because nothing that needs consent is used, no consent banner or “cookie settings” control is provided.

Third-party requests

The pages load two resources from outside services. These requests do not use cookies that I am aware of, but they do share your IP address and browser details with the provider so the file can be delivered:

  • Google Fonts, provided by Google, for the website typefaces.
  • cdnjs, provided by Cloudflare, for the three.js library that draws the 3D laptop.

These providers set their own rules, described in their privacy policies. I do not control them. Links to other websites, such as Gmail, WhatsApp and the ESTORA Suites project, open services that may use cookies of their own.

Managing cookies in your browser

All major browsers let you view, block and delete cookies and site data in their settings. You can also block external fonts and scripts with a browser extension.

Changes

If I add tools that use cookies or similar technologies, I will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent before they run.
